WORK

Clicky

I designed clicky to make ourselves more aware of the longevity of a product. In this day and age, we quickly buy a completely new bag when it's damaged. But these bags, because of their special click system, can be taken apart completely. This allows you to easily replace or adjust a piece. These bags are made from sturdy felt which makes the bags flexible and easy to assemble.
